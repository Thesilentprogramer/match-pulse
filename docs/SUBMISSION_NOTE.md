Fans second-screen a live match, and after cash contests ended that screen often feels empty. The score, the chat, and a fantasy total all shout at once, and a new fan will not ask what any of it means.

Match Pulse is the live log, made readable. Moment cards and a win-chance swing arrive with each ball. Before the over, you and a private pod stake free Pulse Points — not cash, not purchasable, not redeemable — on two or three calls. A companion answers in English, Hinglish, or plain language and may quote only numbers from the balls already received.

Match Summary is one click. The same log becomes a 9:16 pencil film with a voiceover that may speak only numbers already in the match. The player is the summary when the host cannot render.

Live scores try Cricbuzz first, then a budgeted CricAPI board, then the recorded demo, so the screen is never empty.

Built with Cursor cloud agents. The companion uses the Vercel AI SDK when a key is set. A desk voice covers the demo with no key.
